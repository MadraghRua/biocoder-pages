BioCoder · EDP

Agentic AI for molecular design, with a record built for cGMP and cGLP labs.

EDP is an MCP-based AI agent that designs cloning and primer workflows and checks each step against validated tools. It also records why every decision was made, so tech transfer, change control and investigations start from evidence.

Use case
cGMP · tech transfer

A construct moves from R&D to a CDMO for manufacturing

Today
  • The plasmid map is handed over, but the rationale isn't
  • The original designer has often moved on
  • Weeks go to clarification cycles and reverse-engineering
  • When a failure happens, the root cause is a guess
With EDP
  • Every site, primer and junction comes with its reason
  • The validation checks that ran are on record
  • Each change shows what changed and why
  • Investigations trace failures back through the design
The same record supports cGLP study traceability: which construct version and checks each study arm used. EDP isn't a validated GxP system today. It produces the design record your QMS references.
