BioCoder EDP

Agentic AI for molecular design, with a record built for regulated labs

EDP's MCP-based agent designs cloning and primer workflows and checks each step against validated tools. It also records why every decision was made. Labs keep the plasmid map, but usually lose the reasoning behind it. EDP keeps both.

Tech transferThe design rationale goes to the CDMO along with the construct.
Change controlThe record shows what changed in a construct and why.
InvestigationsFailures can be traced back through the design decisions, giving input to CAPA.
cGLP traceabilityEach study arm is tied to its exact construct version.

EDP isn't a validated GxP system today. It produces the design record your QMS references.
